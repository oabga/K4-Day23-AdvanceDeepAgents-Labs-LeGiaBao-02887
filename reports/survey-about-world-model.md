# Survey on World Models

## TL;DR
- World models enhance decision-making and understanding in robotics and AI applications by simulating interactions with environments and predicting object behaviors [1][2].
- Different types of world models—statistical, simulation-based, and hybrid—exhibit distinct methodologies, accuracy, and applications across various fields including healthcare and autonomous systems [3][4].
- Recent advancements in world models are focused on scalability, adaptability, and ethical considerations, emphasizing safe implementations in real-world settings [5].

## Background  
World models are computational frameworks that simulate aspects of the environment to support decision-making in AI systems. Their importance has increased as they enable advanced functionality in robotics, cognitive science, and autonomous systems [1][2]. Foundational work on predictive modeling emphasizes their role in enhancing cognitive capabilities and task execution, leading to refined interactions between agents and their environments [5].

## Types of World Models  
World models can be categorized into statistical models, simulation-based models, and hybrid models. Statistical models leverage data from observed phenomena to make predictions about future states [3]. Simulation-based models provide dynamic environments to evaluate potential outcomes of various actions [6]. Hybrid models combine both approaches by integrating predictive analytics with simulation techniques to achieve higher accuracy [5]. The choice of model type significantly impacts performance, accuracy, and application areas, with specific use cases found across healthcare and autonomous systems [4].

## Applications of World Models  
World models find applications in numerous fields, including gaming and autonomous vehicles. For instance, they are utilized in crisis support systems to gauge the impacts of interventions on user trust [1]. In autonomous driving, models enable efficient decision-making by anticipating environmental changes based on user actions [6]. As these models evolve, they increasingly influence dynamics in human-robot interactions, enhancing training efficiency for AI agents in various simulations [2]. 

## Trends and Open Problems  
Recent trends in world model research focus on scalability and integration across diverse applications [5]. However, challenges persist, particularly regarding adaptability and safe deployment in unknown environments. Ethical considerations in autonomous decision-making also demand attention, calling for robust frameworks that prioritize safety and bias mitigation [5]. Key open questions involve defining effective features for world models and establishing a unified benchmarking system to evaluate their performance across different contexts [7].
